Quick fills:

Seat source League (random) draws from active League members. Re-roll K=0 keeps that draw for the whole run; K>0 swaps every K episodes. The agent learns after every episode. Evaluation interval k freezes it every k episodes and plays n test episodes against every active League member; those results feed the box plot on the Overview tab and are never trained on. Starting stack is the chip buy-in for each seat. Small blind and big blind are posted at the start of each Hold'em hand. The big blind must be at least the small blind, and the starting stack must cover the big blind. Tick Sees all cards on a seat to show that one agent every opponent's hole cards while the rest of the table plays normal poker. Use it to check that a learner improves when nothing is hidden from it, then untick.
